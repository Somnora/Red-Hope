18. **E8's frame bar is judged on quiet pairs** (director answer L (a), 2026-10-29; `e8-market-panel-design.md`, DIRECTOR ANSWER (2026-10-29)). This replaces R2-7's "in every pair" and the E8 brief's item 14 and frame-check Bar. A pair counts only when `uptime`'s 1-minute load, read from a separate shell just before its first window, is under 5. If the editor alone holds that reading at 5 or more (a session started under 5 never reads under 5 at a pair), every pair of a session whose start reading (no Unreal, battery or Build.sh process running) was under 5 counts instead, and the builder says which rule applied. Each scene and resolution needs three counted pairs, and the bar is the median of their three P1 - P0 deltas: at most 1.0 ms for the median frame time and for p95. The `Market` slot (0.2 ms a frame) and the worst rebuild (2 ms) stay hard bars on every counted P1 window. Every pair is reported, counted or not. A scene and resolution that cannot get three counted pairs within 45 minutes is a busy machine for the orchestrator, never a pass and never a director question. E8 only: no other gate's bar changes.
